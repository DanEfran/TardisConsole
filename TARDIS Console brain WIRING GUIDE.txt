 Part 1
 ------
 
Preliminary wiring, initial installation of upgrades...wiring subject to change.
 
 
 - POWER pins:
  - GND: BLACK to Console Reset button (below Panel B, between mouse and keyboard supports)
  - GND: BLACK to patchblock GND BUS
  - 5V: RED to patchblock 5V BUS
  - RESET: BROWN from Console Reset button
  
 - PWM pins:
  - 11: YELLOW to audio player TX
  - 12: GREEN to audio player RX
  - 13: built-in LED (off)
  - GND: BROWN to audio player MODE
  
    - 0, 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 7: R,O,Y,G,B,V,Brown,Black of Rainbow One to patchblock: 
										Black 7: panel meter (+)

 - COMMUNICATION pins
  - 14: PURPLE to audio player RESET
  - 15: YELLOW to audio player ACTIVE
  - 16: RED to Console Major Mode button (below panel B, next to Reset, marked Power)
  - 17: ORANGE from Console Major Mode button
  
 - DIGITAL pins
  - 52, 53: Door Lever
  - 50, 51: Demat Lever
  - 48, 49: Major Mode button
										
  - 39, 41, 43, 45: G, Black; Y, O: Lockout Key; Fast Return Switch
  - 23,25,27,29,21,33,35,37: Black, Brown, R, O, Y, G, B, V Rainbow Two to patchblock:
										Black, Brown, Red 23, 25, 27:demat lights
										Orange, Yellow 29, 31: red light, green light
										Green 33: Plinth Big Square Button
										Blue 35: panel A warning light
										Violet 37: (panel B panel meter) overload light
										
  
 - ANALOG IN pins
  - A0...A4: O, Y, G, B, V, of 5-pin Analog Rainbow Three to patchblock
  - A0: Orange of rainbow Three to BROWN of Speed Knob
  
 
 - PATCHBLOCK
 
  - BUS +/-: RED, BLACK to Audio Power +/-
  - BUS GND: BLACK: to TARDIS Reset Button
  - BUS 5V: resistors to RED, ORANGE, YELLOW of Demat Lights
  - ORANGE, YELLOW, GREEN of rainbow one, to G, B, V of Demat Lights (LOW = ON)
  - BLUE, VIOLET of rainbow one, through resistors, to Panel A Red, Green Lights

 ------
 Part 3
 ------
  
List of active controls
With functions where known, subject to change.
Controls are on Panel B unless otherwise noted.

- Console Reset button: wired to brain's actual RESET line: arduino panic button.
- Console Major Mode button: OFF: TARDIS mode; ON: Rocketship mode.
- Demat Lever: TOGGLE: start TAKEOFF or LANDING (alternate). Debounce 1-2 sec min, enough to allow ON-OFF or OFF-ON gestures as equivalent to TOGGLE (to allow user to reverse polarity of switch's throw)
- Demat Lights: bargraph on and off at start of TAKEOFF and LANDING respectively
- Door Lever: TOGGLE: OPEN DOOR or CLOSE DOOR (alternate). Debounce as per Demat Lever.

- Speed Knob (Panel A): @#@u Ultimate use undecided. Currently controls Panel B Panel Meter and Overload Light

 

 ------
 Part 4
 ------
  
Outputs, modes, events, etc.

- TARDIS (Major) Mode: All sounds are from TARDIS soundset
- Rocketship (Major) Mode: All sounds are from Rocketship soundset
- TAKEOFF (minor mode): Play takeoff sound; animate Demat lights to ON bargraphwise. THEN go to FLIGHT (minor) mode.
- LANDING (minor mode): Play landing sound; animate Demat lights to OFF bargraphwise. THEN go to IDLE (minor) mode.
- IDLE (minor mode): Play idle environmental sound(s)? Blink some blinkenlights?
- FLIGHT (minor mode): Play in-flight environmental sound(s)? Blink some blinkenlights?
- OPEN DOOR (event): Play open door sound, then resume current minor mode.
- CLOSE DOOR (event): Play close door sound, then resume current minor mode.


 ------
 Part 5
 ------
 
Operation notes

- When connecting sound fx board to computer, it must be mostly disconnected from arduino! Even if arduino is unpowered, fx board's reset line (at least) must be disconnected.

- If audio fx board shows "Found 0 files" and won't play sounds or even adjust volume, yet does show "Adafruit FX Sound Board 11/6/14" startup message: check for accidentally disconnected UG pin (or other wiring problems).

- Sketch is TardisConsoleMega2560.ino

- First phase (door and demat levers, animated demat lights, major modes) complete
   and working as of version 19, 4/7/2021.
   
- Next phase: more controls and lights. Cables to panels A and C.
